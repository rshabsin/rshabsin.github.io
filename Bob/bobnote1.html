My wife Mei-O remembers meeting Bob. We went to his house and also met his mom and maybe his dad and younger sister Judy, and since I didn't bring Mei-O to the U.S. until September of 1971, and we left the U.S. in June of 1972 so I could study at Donghai University in Taiwan for a year, I'd guess it was sometime in late 1971 or early 1972 when I last saw him.
