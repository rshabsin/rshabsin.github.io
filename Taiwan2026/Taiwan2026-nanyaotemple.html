彰化南瑤宮 (Changhua Nanyao Temple)
April 13, 2026

The 彰化南瑤宮 (Changhua Nanyao Temple) is a beautiful old temple built in 1738 honoring the goddess 媽祖 (Mazu), the deity of Chinese fishermen and sailors and all who go to sea. Nowadays she is celebrated all over Taiwan, no longer only by seafarers; she is considered the "Heavenly Holy Mother" (天上聖母). Here are some pictures of the temple I took during our visit on April 13th, 2026. The pictures are in no special order.
