Port of Taichung "Unship" Sign
April 12, 2026

Here are the contents of an interesting sign at the Port of Taichung. 卸魚區 (UNSHIP) means "Fish Unloading Area". And while the left side sets out some rules for port workers and visitors, the right side was a complete surprise. The left side reads:

在漁港區域內,不得為下列行為:
一、危害安全及妨礙船舶航行行為。
二、排放有毒物質、有害物質、廢油。
三、排放廢污水或任意投棄廢棄物。
四、採捕或養殖水產動植物,本港區
域未開放垂釣,請勿於碼頭垂釣,
以免發生危險
。 五、其他經主管機關依法公告禁止之
行為。
六、從事水域遊憩活動、放流(生)及辦
理放流(生)相關活動。

translated by Google Translate as:

The following activities are prohibited within the fishing port area:
1. Actions endangering safety or obstructing vessel navigation;
2. Discharging toxic or harmful substances or waste oil;
3. Discharging wastewater or arbitrarily dumping waste;
4. Harvesting or farming aquatic plants and animals.
Fishing is not permitted in this port area;
please do not fish at the pier to avoid danger.
5. Other activities prohibited by law and announced by the competent authority.
6. Engaging in recreational activities in the water, releasing fish (or other marine life),
and organizing related activities.

And here, quite surprisingly, is what it says on the right side:


自殺不能解決問題, 勇敢求救並非弱者,
生命一定可以找到出路。
透過守門123步驟
