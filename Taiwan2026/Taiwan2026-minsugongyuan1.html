The 民俗公園 (Folklore Park)
April 8, 2026

The 民俗公園 was full of activity all day long, and often well into the evening, even after dark. Here are some of the things we saw during our first walk through the park. It's called the Folklore Park because it has a small museum with artifacts from Taiwan's past (which we didn't go in to see, since we'd been through it many times during our past visits). We spent most of our time during this walk checking out the museum buildings.
