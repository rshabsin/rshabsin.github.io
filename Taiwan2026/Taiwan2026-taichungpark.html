The red bridge As we crossed it, we saw a lot of fish and a few turtles in the lake.



Read right to left over the entrance, "湖心亭," is the pavilion's name, the "Heart of the Lake Pavilion."


Those blue things are rental row boats. On weekends and holidays when the park is jumping, you will often find a lot of young people out on the lake.
