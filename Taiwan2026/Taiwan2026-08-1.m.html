Today's breakfast, just here as a place holder to maintain the timeline. It was another typical Zhong Ke Hotel all-you-can-eat buffet breakfast, with, as usual, several new items on the menu this morning. Here are a few of the available selections.
Around 9:30, Zhiwei, with Mingqi riding shotgun, picked up me, Mei-O, Meihui, and Wenxin and, after stopping by a Starbucks drive-thru for a coffee for Zhiwei, we headed to 彰化 (Changhua), about a 30 minute ride from the hotel.
Changhua is the home to 八卦山 (Bagua Mountain) where a Great Buddha statue, guarded by two ferocious-looking lions, sits atop a big hill. We drove through this gate, up to the parking lot at the top, avoiding the long walk up the statue-lined pathway leading to the Buddha with statues like this and this and this guiding you to the Great Buddha.
We all gathered before the Buddha for a selfie taken by Zhiwei. Looking straight ahead over the empty dragon fountain, there was a great view of Changhua below.
Mei-O, Meihui, and I ventured inside the Buddha where we found this altar. Both Mei-O and Meihui lit candles (NT$100, about $3, for two), baibai-ed, asking for blessings for our families, then placed the candles on the altar (the two up front on the left). The three of us then climbed a narrow stairway to the top of the Buddha (where there's a dirty window facing 大佛寺, the "Big Buddha Temple"), viewing dioramas about the life of the Buddha on the various levels as we climbed.
We took a few more family pictures and selfies before leaving the grounds and heading...
