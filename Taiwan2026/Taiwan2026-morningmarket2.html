A Morning Market
April 14, 2026

We didn't use Google maps to find a route to the market, because we didn't know where the market was. I just led our quest by instinct, and maybe some buried-deep-inside memory, and somehow, we found it with no wrong turns or detours. Our route from the hotel looked like this. Exiting the hotel, we turned right and walked a block down 崇德路 to 大連路, then turned right and walked past Meihui's house up 大連路 until we came to 昌平路 (the road I vaguely remembered which started me on this quest). We then turned left on 昌平路, and shortly arrived at the market at the intersection of 昌平路 and 金谷巷. The whole walk was just about a half mile long.

The walk was somewhat hazardous, since in Taiwan, sidewalks are often blocked by businesses and you often have to walk in the street. Both 大連路 and 昌平路 are pretty busy and, when I checked Google Maps after the fact, I found this route along 熱河路 and 崇德二路 which may actually be a little shorter and is definitely much safer. We used it on our second trip to the market on the 16th.

I've documented this on this page for future reference, so next time we return (whenever that may be), we'll know how to get to the market if we want to.
