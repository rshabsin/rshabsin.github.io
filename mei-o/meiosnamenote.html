Mei-O's Name

The modern Pinyin spelling of Mei-O's surname 羅 is actually Luo, not Lo. Her given name, 美娥, should have an apostrophe in place of the dash to separate the two syllables, with the "O" replaced with an "e", rendering the proper Pinyin spelling as Luo Mei'e. Despite this, we've stuck with the original spelling of her name given to us by the U.S. government when we were married in Taiwan (Lo Mei-O), with her first name appearing on all her official documentation (citizenship papers, passport, Social Security card, driver's license, etc.) as Mei-O.
