The Four Tones

The Chinese language is tonal. There are four basic tones in Mandarin (high-level, rising, falling-rising, and falling, numbered one through four, respectively.) A basic sound/syllable pronounced with a different tone will mean something entirely different. For example:

媽1st (high-level)   ma1mother
麻2nd (rising)   ma2hemp
馬3rd (falling-rising)   ma3horse
罵4th (falling)   ma4to scold

Additionally, in some cases, certain combinations of two tones in succession cause the first tone to change. This tonal quality of the language makes it very difficult for foreigners to learn.
