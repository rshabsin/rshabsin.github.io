Aunt Yeva's Name

Although I was told my Aunt Yeva's last name was Milyutina (from the Russian, Милютина), and that's what I used to find so much information about her on the Internet, in many references to her on the web, especially translations by Google Translate, her name comes up Milyutin, without the final 'a'. I don't know enough about the Russian language to know if this is just a Google Translate error, or if the final 'a' sound is silent in her name. And in some cases, her first name is translated as 'Eva', despite the fact that the Russian letter 'Е' makes the 'yeh' (as in 'yet') sound.
