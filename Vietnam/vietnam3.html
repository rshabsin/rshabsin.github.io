Vietnam - Destruction at Tan Son Nhut

My pictures from Tan Son Nhut Air Base in Saigon, Vietnam after a major rocket attack on the base. This was either during the Tet Offensive in January, 1968 or sometime in early April or May of that year, I'm not sure. The morning after the pounding, I walked around and took these pictures of several destroyed barracks that pretty much took direct hits (with several fatalities) and the flight line where many planes and vehicles were destroyed. The enemy had us zeroed in....
