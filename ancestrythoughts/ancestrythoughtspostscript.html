Some Thoughts on My Ancestry - A Postscript

Over the years, especially as I've gotten older, I've though about who and where I came from. I mean my ancestry, as I'm certainly aware I was born and raise in Chicago, Illinois, USA. My parents have very different origins, and, because I never in my youth thought about (or, apparently, cared about) trying to find out where they came from, I've tried to find out whatever I could from the few remaining living relatives I have been in contact with. What I've found out is documented here.

My Father, Leonard Saul Shabsin

I've always been told that my father was born in Chicago on December 27, 1917. His father, George, my grandfather who I knew well and who I remember spending a lot of time with as a kid, was born late in the 19th century; the year 1895 sticks in my mind for some reason.

I've always thought that as a boy, he came over from Europe, and his Shabsin name was something made up by the immigrant-processors of Ellis Island, with no clue as to what it originally may have been. George had three brothers, Max, Henry (called Harry by all), and David.

Growing up in Chicago, I occasionally met Uncle Dave who lived in a south Chicago suburb. He was a tailor and never (to my knowledge) married.

Max I may have met once when we visited him in Kalamazoo, Michigan, when I was probably five or six years old. He had a son, Sherman who married Martee, a very interesting woman (and a Facebook friend of mine) who had grown up in the wilds of Alaska. They had several kids, the only one I'm familiar with is Diana (Deedee) who lives in Wisconsin and is also a Facebook friend of mine.

And Uncle Harry, well, he too was single all his life, living all over the world working for the U.S. government. We're pretty sure he was with the CIA, as most of the time he couldn't tell us just where he was.
