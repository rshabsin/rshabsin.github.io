On Growing Old

In the movie "The Straight Story", when Alvin Straight (played by Richard Farnsworth, above), who had bad eyesight and walked with a cane, was asked, while sitting around a crackling campfire, "So, uh, what's the worst part about being old, Alvin?", he replied,

"Well, the worst part of being old is rememberin' when you was young."
