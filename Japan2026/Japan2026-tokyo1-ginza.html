At the Ginza in Tokyo
October 8, 2026

The Ginza (銀座) is a popular upscale shopping area of Tokyo, with numerous internationally renowned department stores, boutiques, restaurants, and coffeehouses concentrated into a major tourist area. It's kind of a "must see" part of Tokyo, so off we went to look around.

The following pictures were taken between 6:00 and 7:30 on the evening of October 8th. Click on any picture to see it full-size.


We left the hotel and headed down a side street towards the Ginza shopping area.


The street was brightly lit up by the abundance of stores along the way, and filled with people.










We decided to pop into the Mitsukoshi Ginza Department Store where we bought a few snacks then walked around some more checking out the food section where all sorts of tempting goodies were available for purchase.


This picture is perhaps one of the most significant pictures of our trip. While we were walking along the busy street mingling with the crowd, I tripped on some uneven pavement, banging my left arm and right hand on the ground pretty hard. Mei-O and some passersby helped me up. I felt okay, nothing really hurt or bothered me, so we continued on strolling the Ginza
