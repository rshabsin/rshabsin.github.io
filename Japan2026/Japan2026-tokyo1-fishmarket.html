The Tsukiji Nippon Fish Port Market
October 11, 2026

The Tsukiji Nippon Fish Port Market is another popular tourist attraction in Tokyo. It was walkable from our hotel. Teresa, in planning our trip, found the best time to visit it was early in the morning around 7:00 AM before the crowds showed up. She and Steve went early this Sunday morning, but Mei-O and I went much later, around 11:30 when the place was bustling with shoppers. All sorts of seafood were available here, and people were buying snacks and meals from the various stalls that lined the streets. (Steve and Teresa had their breakfast here.) Having already had breakfast at the hotel, we were just window shopping, observing the merchandise and the people, not buying anything (although it was tempting.) Here are a bunch of pictures of the market. I wish I could've taken more pictures of the seafood displays the stalls had out, but the crowds kept me away.
