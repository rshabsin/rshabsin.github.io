About Us
Hi! We're Rick and Mei-O (pronounced Mayo, appropriately enough, just like the Mayo Clinic) Shabsin from Rochester, Minnesota (home of the Mayo Clinic). We maintain this website just for fun, using only a simple editor (Notepad++), Photoshop and GIMP, my Google Pixel 7 phone's camera, and an HP scanner. It's served off a DigitalOcean cloud server maintained by our son Chris.

Mei-O and I have have lived in Rochester since January of 1984 when I came here to work for IBM as a software engineer. Before that, we lived in Warren, Ohio where, after I got fired from my job as an air traffic controller by President Reagan for participating in the air traffic controllers' strike in August of 1981, I attended school at Youngstown State University, earning a B.S. in Mathematics with a Computer Science minor in December, 1983. We have one son, Chris, who lives in the Boston area, having graduated from the Massachusetts Institute of Technology (MIT) with a degree in Computer Science and Engineering in 1995. On June 9, 2012, he married Dana at Purity Spring Resort in eastern New Hampshire, and on August 24, 2017, they brought our amazing little granddaughter, Lydia, into the world! Then on June 18, 2019, our grandson Max joined the family!

What We Did/Do
On August 2, 2013, I retired from Celestica, a global technology services company headquartered in Toronto, Canada, where I worked at their Rochester, Minnesota, site as a Software Engineer/Senior Application Developer. After 29 and a half years of designing and coding manufacturing and engineering-related applications, I plan to remain retired!

On March 31, 2020, after 17 years, Mei-O retired from her Rochester Public School District job in Student Nutrition Services at Sunset Terrace Elementary School.

Chris is a software engineer working for Google in Cambridge, Massachusetts. Dana is also a software engineer. She works for Cargurus, also in Cambridge.
